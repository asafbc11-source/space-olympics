SHACKLETON RESCUE

Drive the Lunar Med Pod down into Shackleton Crater. Avoid the hazards, climb with the upgrades, reach the injured astronaut at square 100.

Turn
Player 1
Player 1 starts at the base (square 1). Roll to begin the rescue!

Hazards (snakes) → Med Pod upgrades (ladders)

How to play

Take turns rolling the dice. Land on a hazard and you slide down the snake, because a standard vehicle would fail there. Land on a Med Pod upgrade and you climb the ladder. Each hazard has an upgrade in the same color that solves it. You must roll the exact number to reach 100.
